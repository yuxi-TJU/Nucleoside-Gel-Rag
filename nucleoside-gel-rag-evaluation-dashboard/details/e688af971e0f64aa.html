This *syn* conformation is highly favorable for the formation of G-quartets. Unlike unmodified guanosine, which often requires specific cations to stabilize the G-quartet core, 8-substituted guanosines are known to self-assemble into hydrogels more readily due to this pre-organized *syn* geometry.
