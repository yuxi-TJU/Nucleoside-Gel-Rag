### Analysis

8-Bromoguanosine is a well-studied derivative of guanosine. Guanosine and its derivatives are classic building blocks for supramolecular gels due to their ability to form G-quartets via Hoogsteen hydrogen bonding. These G-quartets further stack into columnar structures, which can entangle to form a gel network. The introduction of a bromine atom at the C8 position of the guanine base significantly alters the conformational landscape of the nucleoside. Specifically, the bulky bromine atom forces the glycosidic bond into a *syn* conformation. This *syn* preference is highly favorable for the formation of G-quartets, as it facilitates the specific hydrogen-bonding patterns required for self-assembly.
