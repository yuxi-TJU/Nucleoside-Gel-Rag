The decisive supramolecular interaction that produces the cytidine hydrogel is Ag⁺-mediated base pairing (C–Ag⁺–C coordination), which, together with the borate-sugar network, yields a percolating, self-supporting gel. Uracil lacks the N4 amino group and the electronic pattern required for stable Ag⁺ bridging; consequently the second cross-linking motif is absent.
