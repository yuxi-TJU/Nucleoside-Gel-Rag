### Analysis of Deoxyguanosine (dG)
Deoxyguanosine (dG) is a well-documented nucleoside known for its ability to self-assemble into G-quartet structures. In the presence of specific alkali metal cations, these quartets stack via $\pi-\pi$ interactions to form long, fibrous supramolecular polymers that can entrap solvent to form a hydrogel.
