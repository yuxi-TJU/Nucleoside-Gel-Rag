Output

The target molecule (2-FA) is a close structural analog of the non-gelling 2-aminoadenosine and 2′-deoxy-2-aminoadenosine references (chemical distances 2.50–2.62). Both of those compounds remain fully soluble and produce no hydrogel under essentially identical conditions (1.7–5 wt % in PBS or water, heat–cool).  

The 2′-fluoro substituent is known to promote gelation only when paired with an isoguanine or guanine base (see FisoGd and NH₂G references); the adenine-like nucleobase present here lacks the complementary H-bonding pattern and carbonyl required for stable G-quartet-like assemblies. PBS, being a buffered aqueous medium without added chaotropic or kosmotropic salts that might otherwise stabilize fibers, does not overcome this limitation.  

Consequently the molecule is expected to remain a clear solution after cooling, exactly as observed for the nearest non-fluorinated adenine analogs.
