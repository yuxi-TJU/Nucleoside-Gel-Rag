Given that the borate-mediated gelation mechanism requires the cis-diol (2',3'-OH) to form the diester bridge, the substitution of the 2'-OH with an amino group prevents the formation of the necessary covalent cross-links required to stabilize the G-quartet assembly into a macroscopic hydrogel. Therefore, it is expected that this molecule will not form a stable gel under these conditions.
