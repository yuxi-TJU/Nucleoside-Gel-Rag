2.  **Solvent Interaction:** Chloroform (CHCl₃) is a non-polar to moderately polar solvent that is often used in the study of nucleoside-based gelators. The self-assembly is driven by the stacking of the uracil bases and the interdigitation of the palmitoyl chains, forming a fibrous network that traps the solvent.

### Conclusion
Based on the structural characteristics and established behavior of 5'-O-acyl nucleosides in organic media, 5'-O-Palmitoyluridine is expected to form a gel in chloroform.
