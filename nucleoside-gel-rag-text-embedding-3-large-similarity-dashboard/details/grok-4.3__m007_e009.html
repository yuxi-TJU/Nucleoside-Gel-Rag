GB hydrogels made with 0.5 equiv KB(OH)₄ have remained transparent for over a year.

Borate forms diesters with the ribose cis-diol, dimerising guanosine and positioning it for G₄·K⁺ quartet stacking. The borate diesters act as covalent cross-links, while optimally sized K⁺ ions template and stabilise the stacked quartets. π–π stacking plus hydrogen bonding between quartets yields long nanofibres that entangle into a highly stable network; removal of either borate or K⁺ disrupts this dual stabilisation and the gel fails.
