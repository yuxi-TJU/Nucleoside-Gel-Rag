TAcG alone is a nongelator. In G/TAcG mixtures (40–60 % TAcG) the hydrogels stay transparent and crystalline-free > 1 year, with storage modulus and yield stress decreasing as TAcG fraction rises.

Acetyl groups sterically cap guanosine stacks and introduce hydrophobic cross-links. When co-assembled with G, TAcG disrupts the regular G₄ stacking that drives crystallisation, yielding shorter but thicker fibres. Hydrophobic acetyl contacts act as additional, temperature-resistant cross-links, raising gel-to-sol transition temperature (~63 °C for 50 / 50 mix) and preventing crystal growth. Increasing TAcG therefore tunes mechanical softness while dramatically extending gel lifetime.
