As seen in Reference 3 with 8-Aza-2′-deoxyguanosine (z8Gd), the replacement of N8 with nitrogen diminishes the basicity of the ring nitrogens involved in Hoogsteen hydrogen-bonding. This disruption prevents the stable formation of the quartet/pentaplex stacks required for gelation in aqueous media. Even though z8isoGd retains the 2′-deoxy modification (which typically promotes hydrophobic interactions), the electronic disruption caused by the 8-aza modification is a dominant factor that destabilizes the supramolecular assembly required for a percolating fiber network. Therefore, it is expected that z8isoGd will fail to form a stable hydrogel under the specified conditions.
