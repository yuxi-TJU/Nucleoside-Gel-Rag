3.  **Conclusion:**
    While Uridine satisfies the borate-binding requirement due to its *cis*-diol, it lacks the specific nucleobase functionality (the C4-amino group) necessary to form the Ag⁺-mediated base pairs that provide the structural backbone for the nanofiber network. Without these orthogonal interactions, the system cannot form a self-supporting hydrogel.
