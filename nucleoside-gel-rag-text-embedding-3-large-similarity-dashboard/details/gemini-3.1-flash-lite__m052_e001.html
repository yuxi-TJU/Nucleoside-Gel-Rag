When cooled immediately, a transparent, self-supporting hydrogel formed (G' ~700 Pa for 72 mM gel) that appeared stable for weeks at rt. When kept at 90°C for 15 min before cooling, a viscous solution was obtained instead of a gel.

The 2',3'-diol of 5'-IG reacts with borate from KB(OH)₄ to form anionic guanosine-borate (GB) diesters. These esters self-assemble via hydrogen bonds and π-π stacking to form G4-wires, which entangle into a hydrogel network. However, 5'-IG can undergo an intramolecular cyclization where N3 displaces the 5'-iodide leaving group, forming 5'-deoxy-N3,5'-cycloguanosine (5'-cG). This cyclization is facilitated by a syn conformation of the nucleoside and deprotonation of N1H (pKa ~9) at the gel pH (~8.6). The resulting 5'-cG lacks an N1-H hydrogen bond donor, making its hydrogen bonding pattern incompatible with stable G4-quartet formation. Its in situ formation thus disrupts the gel network, leading to 'self-destruction.
